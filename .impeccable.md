## Design Context

### Users
Mobile-first players in East Africa who check hatches, earnings, and deposits.

### Brand Personality
Warm, magical, collectible.

### Aesthetic Direction
An old-school dragon-and-dinosaur collecting game with a deep-blue theme, asymmetrical compositions, and clear, dependable money actions.

### Design Principles
- Make the egg-to-dinosaur collecting loop the most memorable part of the experience.
- Use playful retro character art and angled shapes without sacrificing legibility.
- Keep balances, deposit details, and withdrawal destinations calm and explicit.
- Design for a narrow mobile viewport first, then expand into an intentional desktop composition.
